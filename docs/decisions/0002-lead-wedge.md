# 0002 — Lead wedge: accessibility-first

- Date: 2026-05-12
- Status: Accepted

## Context

The build plan describes three viable wedges: accessibility-first (Wedge A), builders/creators (Wedge B), power users craving real context (Wedge C). Generalist positioning is a graveyard; one must lead the marketing message, even though the product serves all three.

Accessibility-first is structurally defensible because: (1) ~250M+ severe-motor-impairment users globally with a larger penumbra; (2) Existing tools (Dragon, Apple Voice Control, Windows Speech Recognition) are pre-LLM; (3) Scribe v2's atypical-speech support and IVC personalization are uniquely valuable to this cohort; (4) Incumbents (Apple, Google, Microsoft, Anthropic) consistently underinvest in this market.

## Decision

Lead marketing, demo persona, and feature prioritization with **accessibility-first**. The product itself serves builders and context-craving power users equally; the *message* leads with the accessibility wedge.

## Consequences

- Demo videos use accessibility personas (hands-busy, motor-impaired, atypical speech).
- Phase 12 (accessibility depth) is treated as first-class V3 work, not optional.
- We invest in disability-community partnerships from Phase 0 onward (§30 open Q9).
- Marketing is dual-message: "Talk to your computer. It does anything." + sub-message "For people who can't or won't use a keyboard."
- Accessibility-free pricing tier exists by V3 launch (Phase 17.7).

## Recovery

If after 50+ beta users this wedge fails to produce strong qualitative endorsement, pivot the public marketing to Wedge B (builders) while keeping accessibility features as a strong secondary message. The product surface doesn't change.
